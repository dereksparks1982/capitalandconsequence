## Completed source slices this checkpoint
1. **NPC memory foundation**: `npc-memory.js` with verified versus unverified observations, importance-based retrieval, bounded per-profile records, and reflection. `npc-dialogue.js` uses private visitor-scoped impressions without turning gossip or the player's text into established world truth.
2. **Social temperament**: `npc-personality.js` adds individual patience, pride, politeness, discretion, curiosity and honesty, with deterministic approach/brush-off decisions. Rudeness is personal, never automatically derived from race, origin, or social group.
3. **Card-only dialogue UI**: room visitors in `npc-ai.js` are clickable entries; `npc-character-card.js` opens **their own card above the room**, with adult male/female generic silhouettes until approved portraits exist. All spoken text, transcript and free-form field are **inside the card**, with no topics, choice menus, suggested prompts, or in-room dialogue widgets. Escape closes only the card, preserving the actual city room. Departing NPCs close their card; leaving the room closes it.
4. **Curated free-form answers and guarded ignorance**: `npc-scripted-talk.js` gives different named residents different responses to a freely typed question about the Star Club, and reacts with admitted ignorance to unsupported questions or date-inappropriate technology. An available optional local model can provide broader conversational phrasing. `npc-dialogue.js` supplies date, individual background, language, temperament, relationships and memory; its outputs are **text, not commands or authoritative facts**. An external model could still hallucinate, so hard factual validation and authored knowledge expansion remain open.
5. **One coherent resident-list source**: retired fixed, always-present `location.people` dialogue pages in `app.js`. Actual room visitors appear only if the saved simulation shows them present in the same **room**, with no teleportation by panel redraw. Added Anna Petrovna to the named simulated roster, bringing the first resident set to **eleven**.
6. **Initial weekly schedules**: `npc-weekly-schedule.js` describes usual day and weekend patterns for those eleven residents. `npc-calendar.js` consumes those plans for current occupancy and `npc-itinerary.js` uses the **same** plans for learned/public routine approximations. The actual NPC can deviate through existing utilities and destination/room-permission rules; contact cards cannot see private intentions. These are still four broad calendar periods, **not** a fully minute-granular realistic itinerary system.
