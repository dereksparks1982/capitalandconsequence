# Capital & Consequence v0.7.32-dev | Edmund Wardman's Custody Crisis

**Source changes on `main`; not a tested or accepted Linux release.**

Edmund's patient-ward choices now include defiance, attempted escape with property damage, an assault, peaceful cooperation and a request for an outside medical opinion. All actions are location-gated, consume ordinary world time and require a witnessed entry. The new custody record alters family pressure and workplace trust.

A proposed experimental lobotomy requires **at least two documented serious misconduct incidents, including violence**, with a cumulative score of eight. It never follows merely from family pressure or a single mistake. There is a three-calendar-day warning and review interval during which cooperation, a second opinion, police evidence or a lawful confinement appeal may reduce or eliminate the risk. Outcomes are seeded by shared campaign ID, persist once decided and cannot be rerolled by repeated clicks. If performed, the alternate-history operation ends Edmund's playable career permanently; a rejected operation does not erase his independent appeal rights.

**Historical note:** The game's 1878 lobotomy is intentionally fictional. Lobotomy developed in actual history in the 1930s; no surgical procedures are represented or advised.

Integration: `wardman-custody.js`, six patient-ward actions, automated day checks, private casebook status, a local regression test and package version 0.7.32. No approved map, pin or room geometry changes. **Full Node regression, desktop smoke and packaged Linux tests remain outstanding; no GitHub Actions used.**
