# Washington Visitability and Map Location Expansion
**Capital & Consequence · November 1, 2026 · alternate-history rule**

## Guiding rule, approved by the creator
A place printed on the fixed Washington map **can be visited in any game year**. The game's 1878 opening date must never hide a location just because the real-world structure was built in 1893, 1910, 1919, 1933, or later. This includes the D.C. World's Fair, both airports, the kennel track and the speedway. Private or staff-only *rooms* may still require permission, invitations, admission, or missions. Historical dates are educational context only, **not a gameplay prohibition**.

The approved guide image must not be repainted, replaced or moved. Each new registered city landmark can have its own numbered pin over the image, with coordinates adjustable using Edit Pins. Entries without verified pin positions should say **provisional**, and must not silently shift existing pins.

## Place registration workflow
1. Locate and name a printed building on the approved image.
2. Add a city-registry entry with unique ID and public default room, and a provisional editable pin if needed.
3. Use the **right-button map pan**, mouse wheel, Edit Pins and manual road painter to position its actual doorway and street access.
4. Leave private rooms restricted, but never block public location entry by historical year.
5. Later connect appropriate services (tickets, buying/selling, employment, court cases, markets, train station, research, food, etc.).

The new Ford's Theatre entry and removal of mapped-location year locks are **implemented**. Everything in the expansion shortlist remains **planning** until its city registry, coordinates and usable room scene have been built.
