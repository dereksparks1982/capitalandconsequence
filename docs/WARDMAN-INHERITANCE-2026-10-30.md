# Capital & Consequence 0.7.35-dev | Wardman Family Settlement

A real two-stage paper trail now exists in Edmund's asylum story. Once Edmund has compared the disputed director's duty register and a family petition has been documented, an addressed letter arrives at the asylum reception desk. It describes a family settlement through which a trustee could control his income or voting interests following a declaration of incapacity. This raises a *possible financial motive* for institutional pressure, NOT guilt in the asylum murder.

Edmund can request an independent reception clerk's signed verification of the incoming-mail entry on a later day. The letter itself gives no immediate corroboration; the authenticated receipt contributes two points to the existing institutional hearing evidence system and records only what the clerk could verify. No family names or hidden murder-role truth are leaked. Correspondence enters Edmund's private history and book, not Sam's police casebook.

Access checks: only Edmund at the existing asylum reception, active day phase, not confined, no developer room, after the revised duty book and family petition. Time costs are 10 minutes for each of the two actions. No duplicated evidence, fabricated money or extra Washington pin.
