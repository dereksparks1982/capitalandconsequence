## Actual gameplay delivered
The existing Le Paon d’Or paid door shift now writes its independently verifiable completed contact into an extra save-backed door book. Each report keeps the original incident ID, date, encounter type, response and existing paid wage. The book never supplies another wage or resets a character's stories.

When the actual visitor situation is a courier delivery, **accept custody** creates a wrapped parcel at the doorman post, with no usable goods and no claim about its unknown contents. Calling the floor manager instead does not secretly give Eli the parcel. A custody parcel stays with Eli until he spends five actual Washington-night minutes handing it to the floor manager; the register then records the transfer, preserving custody history and making duplicate transfers impossible.

After completing all four physically paid entrance contacts for a single night, Eli can file a five-minute nightly door report. It details the contacts actually completed, manager referrals, tense visitors, routine responses and any parcel still in Eli's custody. It does not invent manager approval, a reward, earned criminal contacts, or automatic promotion. Incomplete nights are not falsely reported as complete.

The controls are added inside the original nightclub door panel without moving its approved geometry or changing the owner Lucien Étienne Delacroix, access permissions, or Ely's established employment status. All state remains profile-local.
