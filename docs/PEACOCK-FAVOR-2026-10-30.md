# Capital & Consequence v0.7.34-dev | The Envelope at the Peacock

Eli Crowe now has a real, three-night branching main-story mission at his existing nightclub post. Two paid visitor contacts unlock an encounter with a nervous patron and a sealed envelope. On a later night Eli can return the envelope, lodge it with management or sell it to a stranger for $2.50. A further night's witnessed follow-up records a different outcome for each decision.

The player has no omniscient knowledge of the envelope's contents. No secret evidence or police warrant is invented. Every action is restricted to the real nightclub room during Washington Night, requires charged game-clock time, and is recorded once in Eli's private history and mission journal. Sale proceeds go only to personal cash. The original Washington artwork, map locations, coordinates and room geometry remain unchanged.

Edmund's real institutional commitment hearing, confinement or clinical review now counts as an earned chapter-IV milestone. Mere suspicion without a recorded hearing does not. Later stories are still marked as unimplemented rather than pretended playable.

Source regression coverage: tests/peacock-favor-story.cjs and tests/peacock-favor-wiring.cjs, plus updated four-campaign checks. No GitHub Actions. Not an accepted playable release; full Linux and Electron testing remain pending.
