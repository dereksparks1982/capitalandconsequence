## New real passenger loop
- `city.js` adds a **Passenger Pier** room to the existing, visitable Potomac River Fishing Bank (no new city-map pin, no printed artwork change). It also registers two **unmapped**, directory-hidden physical contexts: passenger vessel cabins/deck and the distant port reached. It does not modify the other 40 fixed map pins.
- `merchant-passenger-sea.js` books passage **only if Charles is physically standing in the Passenger Pier room**, pays the exact quoted price from **personal cash** and records a real passenger voyage plus currentLocation `passengerVessel/cabin`. The trip starts with a labeled **approximate two-day** Potomac/Chesapeake connection to Baltimore before the existing mapped ocean voyage. Each destination is a city/port already present in the shipping lanes; these tickets are **passenger access, not proof of an undiscovered trade market**. Schooner and steamship speeds rely on previously defined estimated navigation days.
- The saved voyage stores exact departure/arrival **worldMinute** values, route coordinates, fare, vessel type, legs and ETA, independent of company cash, owned freight ship accounts and unsent inventory. There is no offline time advancement. Arrival is credited **once**, only after the actual saved minute reaches the scheduled minute.
- `merchant-passenger-ui.js` and CSS display fare quotation, a moving marker over the **original** EcoClipper bitmap, progress, return date, daily report summary and the existing time interface. The player explicitly advances time using the app's authoritative `advanceWorldSimulation` boundaries, retains normal health, world events and report transitions, and can resume/sleep/eat/drink on the ship. The full maritime map also adds a distinct passenger icon alongside existing owned commercial ships.
- On genuine arrival, `currentLocation` becomes the hidden foreign harbour with six small **real player** sectors and an actual saved player coordinate. WASD/arrows/adjacent-tile walking and switching to another area each cost one game minute through the existing clock. Solid walls and water block movement. The destination cannot be reached by simply clicking the world map.
- The harbour offers read-only accurate docked owned vessels and Federal Electric consignment register. No invented shops, random NPCs, customs fees, rooms for rent, goods or money.
- Charles can book and pay for the return crossing from the actual foreign harbour. After that voyage really elapses, he reappears at the **original Potomac Passenger Pier**, not his desk, office or an arbitrary Washington location. Return has its own ticket price, feeder days, receipt, and departure/arrival record. The ordinary Washington map and city-room entry are blocked during maritime absence.
- `medical-records.js` adds a separately billed **maritime** exhaustion setting: $4.50 personal shipboard/foreign-harbour care and two hours unconscious. Recovery stays physically aboard/in the foreign port; it does **not teleport** Charles to Washington hospital. `character-needs.js` distinguishes cabin sleeping from sleeping at home. Paid galley meal ($0.50), water ($0.10), and cabin rest consume actual game time and use existing hunger/thirst/energy effects and cooldowns.
- `app.js` is the **only** passenger mutation gateway for booked travel and local walking: it calls the existing authoritative world simulation and saves after each accepted player action, including a normal daily report workflow. Ordinary map/room traversal and desk-return shortcuts cannot bypass physical voyages.
