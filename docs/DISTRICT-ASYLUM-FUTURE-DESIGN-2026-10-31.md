## Story inspiration, without spoilers

Draw **atmosphere and themes**, not scene-for-scene plots, from **Shutter Island (2010)** and **American Horror Story, Season 2: Asylum**: oppressive institutions, patient vulnerability, dubious or coercive treatments, secrets, guarded areas, conflicting testimony, investigations and uncertainty about authority. **Do not record or expose Shutter Island plot twists, identities or its ending** in game writing, development summaries or conversations: the owner has not finished watching the film. Do not copy identifiable plot structures, dialogue or characters.

## Established world and characters

- Alternate-history **Washington, D.C., beginning around 1878**, retaining actual 19th-century grounding where sensible. The game may deliberately bring some later medical/institutional ideas forward by several decades under the owner's explicit alternate-history permission. Later-era inventions are **fictional early developments**, not falsely labeled real 1878 history.
- The playable orderly **Edmund Wardman** is the unmarried black sheep of the influential Wardman family. His **unnamed older brother** runs the fictional **District Asylum** and assigns Edmund a low-ranking attendant/orderly role. Do **not** invent the brother's name without owner approval.
- The District Asylum is **not** the historic Government Hospital for the Insane/St. Elizabeths, nor the prior exploratory Blackwood investment concept, nor the separate Soviet-set **Moroz Asylum** project.
- **Samuel “Sam” Harlan** is the rookie patrol officer who might legitimately encounter or investigate the same crime. His authority and patient-record access have limits; he does not become an omnipotent detective by entering the location.
- Preserve the already present ward-duty, records, petition, family-settlement and institutional-hearing code/design work where applicable. This note must not erase or contradict established saved progression and source systems. Cross-profile knowledge must come from legitimate in-world information, not from player-only journals.

## Institutional operations and disturbing subjects

Build a believable workplace before horror events. Orderly duties, meals, patient care, cleaning, shift reports, supervisors, visitor times, patient rights and limitations, guard/staff rotations, records and correspondence should form the living baseline.

Potential dark subjects for **responsible fictional depiction**: isolation, restraints, coercive hydrotherapy/cold-water punishment, inappropriate sedation, neglect, abusive staff, forged diagnoses, suppressed complaints, disputed commitment orders and experimental procedures. Portray allegations as allegations until proven; distinguish historically documented practices from imaginary/alternate-timeline developments, and represent institutional cruelty as serious abuse rather than an automatic gameplay benefit.

A **lobotomy** is a potential **rare, grave and irreversible** Edmund outcome in the game's alternate history. The historical real-world procedure is later than 1878; its early appearance here must be explicitly fictional. Do not trigger it as a random punishment for ordinary dialogue or as an unavoidable plot beat. Require an earned, evidenced causal chain, opportunities to contest commitment and meaningful consequences.

## Wardman family conflict and possible involuntary commitment

Edmund might uncover misconduct, clash with his brother, make enemies, sabotage himself or become a convenient target of family pressure. His family may attempt to petition for confinement or transfer control of his affairs and property. Their motive may range from paternalistic self-justification to deliberate exploitation. **Do not lock in the brother as villain or a preselected killer.**

Track legitimate records and contested evidence: duty logs, alleged incidents, witness testimony, evaluations, financial petitions, hearings, visitors, correspondence and who actually has access to each item. False accusations must emerge from in-world events, not an arbitrary game-over. Possible paths include exposing wrongdoing, losing credibility, contesting confinement, becoming a patient, appeal, escape with subsequent legal consequences, or extreme irreversible treatment.

The family settlement paper trail already has a source slice (see `docs/WARDMAN-INHERITANCE-2026-10-30.md`). Future work should expand rather than duplicate it.

## One asylum murder, two perspectives, several possible truths

The player has proposed **one actual death inside District Asylum**, with access from Edmund's and potentially Sam's careers in the same shared world. If a case is generated or started, select and freeze its causal truth **before** players gather evidence; preserve immutable incident/victim IDs, timeline and provenance.

**Possible suspects or causes, not canon solutions:**
- A patient.
- A doctor, orderly or other staff member.
- Edmund's brother.
- An outsider slipping through a legitimately constructed service/sewer route, perhaps motivated by revenge.
- Edmund, or even Sam, **only when the actual simulation/player actions and timeline justify it**, never as a hidden forced twist.

**Edmund view:** observes rounds, behavior and local records; may preserve or corrupt evidence, report concerns, risk retaliation, and decide whom to trust. He cannot instantly read privileged case files or know the killer.

**Sam view:** needs a lawful report, assignment, personal lead or actual witnessed event before involvement; may interview available people, follow admissible clues, request access, and use authorized morgue findings. A rookie does not receive blanket search/arrest powers. His Casebook reveals only facts he has genuinely learned.

Witnesses can lie or remember imperfectly, but the case must remain **fair, logically solvable and internally consistent**. A sewer lead requires verifiable route, physical traces, timing, access records and a reason for the outsider's presence. Do not retroactively change who committed the crime because the player guesses. Do not propagate hidden facts between private books or independent save profiles without legitimate shared-world events.
