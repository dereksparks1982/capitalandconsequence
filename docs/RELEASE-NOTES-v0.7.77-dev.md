# Capital & Consequence v0.7.77-dev | District Asylum everyday service register
November 1, 2026. Source-only development; not an owner-accepted Ubuntu release.

## The new ordinary orderly ledger
The existing District Asylum ordinary duty engine now sends each real completed, filed, paid assignment to a second register inside Edmund’s actual orderly-station scene. The register records only the original verified assignment ID, type, response, date and existing wage. There are no additional wage payments, invented diagnoses, imaginary patients or new manager privileges.

When the actual `supplies` task is answered by submitting a supply request, the filed duty creates a corresponding outstanding stores requisition. A mere inventory check does not create an order. On subsequent Washington days Edmund may record a five-minute follow-up from the orderly station. After two full in-game days, he can refer the unresolved request to the ward supervisor, again taking five minutes. The item remains recorded as **not received**, regardless of paperwork; no counterfeit goods appear in an institution inventory.

Once all three daily ordinary assignments have been completed and filed, Edmund may submit a five-minute end-of-shift handover that includes references to that day’s real services and any requests opened that day. It does not imply that the unnamed asylum director has signed off or that an unverified institutional complaint is true.

All actions are profile-local, save-backed, duplicate-safe, and restricted to Edmund’s established existing duties and the real orderly station. No map geometry, district access, hospital morgue plot, family name, murder evidence, politics or room art is changed.
