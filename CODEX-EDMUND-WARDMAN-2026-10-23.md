# Character Codex: Edmund Wardman

**Project:** Capital & Consequence  
**Status:** Agreed character direction; documentation only, not a claim that gameplay is complete  
**Recorded:** October 23, 2026  
**First selected:** October 22, 2026

## Identity

- **Full name: Edmund Wardman.** The playable fourth protagonist is Edmund, not an unnamed character simply called "Wardman."
- **Surname:** Wardman, inspired by research into historical Washington developer Harry Wardman. Harry is historical inspiration, **not** an established character or brother in the game's 1878 family. No fictional family relationship to the real Harry Wardman is established.
- **Family standing:** Edmund is the **black sheep of the wealthy, influential Wardman family**. The surname brings expectations and scrutiny, not an automatic privileged job.
- **Brother:** Edmund's brother **runs District Asylum**. The brother's personal name has **not** yet been selected; do not invent one.
- **Institution:** **District Asylum** is the planned fictional Washington asylum associated with the playable Wardman story. It is distinct from the historical Government Hospital for the Insane, which exists separately in the city registry. Older notes discussing Blackwood Asylum were exploratory and must not silently replace the selected District Asylum name.

## Opening position

- Edmund's brother assigns him a **low-status orderly/attendant job** at District Asylum because he is considered the family disappointment, rather than granting him a management post.
- He is a working employee subject to ward and supervisory duties, patient assistance, supplies, upkeep, routine records, and the institution's normal workday. This starting position gives him neither ownership nor automatic administrative authority.
- **Edmund has his own desk**, like each of the four playable protagonists. It anchors scheduling, personal affairs, papers and records appropriate to his entry-level position.
- During off-duty time he can live an independent personal life in the shared Washington city, not just work at the asylum.

## Possible outcomes, not guarantees

Edmund might remain an orderly, become a respected attendant, pursue further professional training, enter administration, challenge his brother's control, ultimately acquire influence or control of the asylum, or leave the institution entirely. Family rivalry and institutional politics should have persistent consequences, without scripting a compulsory rise.

## Four protagonists and the shared world

1. **Charles “Charlie” Whitcomb (businessman):** owns **Federal Electric** from the beginning; initially manufactures modest electrical components.
2. **Samuel “Sam” Harlan (patrolman):** begins as a low-ranking beat cop, walks his beat without an assigned horse or car, performs routine police work and desk paperwork, and may pursue independent investigations outside his ordinary duties.
3. **Elias “Eli” Crowe (criminal/doorman):** begins at the bottom as a nightclub doorman, with his own desk and an open-ended life; criminal success is optional, not guaranteed.
4. **Edmund Wardman (orderly):** black sheep of the Wardman family, assigned an orderly job under his brother at **District Asylum**, with his own desk.

Every route participates in Washington's common world and NPC ecology; existing separate-save implementations do not yet constitute one synchronized cross-profile simulation.
