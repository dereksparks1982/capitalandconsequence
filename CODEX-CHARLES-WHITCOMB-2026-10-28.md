# Character Codex: Charles “Charlie” Whitcomb
**Project:** Capital & Consequence  
**Canonical playable protagonist:** Charles “Charlie” Whitcomb  
**Career key:** `industrialist`  
**Updated:** 28 October 2026  
**Status:** Approved 1878 opening character and initial gameplay; later industrial expansion remains an unfinished simulation.

## Starting life and ownership
Charlie **already owns** Federal Electric on January 2, 1878. He is **not** a newly hired factory employee and does not start with an unearned industrial empire. His modest Washington component works initially manufactures telegraph and early electrical apparatus, contacts, relays, insulated wire and related fittings. An incandescent-lamp business is a later technological and licensing possibility, not a first-day product.

The initial factory crew consists of **Charlie plus his one paid assistant, Sullivan**. The owner works rather than automatically hiring a management team. Sullivan earns $6 per five-day production week; Saturday is for deliveries and payroll. Sullivan rents a room at the factory for 25¢ per week. Company and personal money are different accounts, and purchases or withdrawals must be recorded.

Charlie has his own executive desk and records, factory production access and ordinary access to Washington according to time and room permissions. He keeps a **fully opened, flat paper copy** of the unchanged Washington In and Out Guide on his desk, without a frame, folds, added pins or a replacement artwork. Clicking the paper map opens the full interactive Washington map. The original map stays fixed for all game years.

Federal Electric's **Washington pin is black from the beginning** because Charlie already owns it. Buying a small parcel of shares in another company does not by itself confer company ownership or change its pin color.

## Character freedom
Charlie can work as an ordinary proprietor, improve production, seek contracts and personal relationships, or acquire more companies if he earns the means. He has **no guaranteed presidency, monopoly, wealth or industrial destiny**. Business ambitions and personal life both remain player-directed.

## Industrial holdings and transportation
The October 28 v0.6.90-dev source adds initial acquisition/management for a **fictional steel/iron rolling works**, a **regional railroad with passenger and freight services**, and **merchant shipping**. Their finances are separate; steel rails may be sold to an owned railway; the railroad and cargo vessels can transport actual Federal Electric inventory. The merchant fleet has individual ships, daily upkeep, damage, repair, insurance, region-dependent loss hazards and geographically verified/merchant-discovered trade opportunities. Cargo routes are not all unlocked at the beginning.

Fishing craft, fishing meets, and an extremely rare legendary sturgeon are available recreational/commercial development paths. Skill 100 has a **0.5% chance to hook** a legendary fish on an eligible cast, with landing still uncertain. A caught record fish can be mounted and reported in the Metropolitan Ledger.

## Skills
Charlie has a personal saved 0–100 skill record in **Journal → Skills**, part of the full **60-skill registry** approved for all four playable protagonists. His business/manufacturing skill baselines are higher than unrelated professions. Skills improve from performed tasks rather than a global character level; many listed skills currently await implemented activities. Skills are not claims of a historical professional certification or a predetermined personality.

## Securities and broker
Charlie may view period-listed securities at the **physical stockbroker's office**. **Only the broker executes stock orders**. A relationship must be earned through distinct visits before **telegraph instructions from the executive office** become available. No initial telephone trading. Each stock has finite issued and publicly offered shares; company cash and personal portfolio funds remain separate.

## Boundaries
No new property pin may be assigned to Charlie's newly purchased mill or railway without an owner-approved map location. The exact original Washington map art, saved location coordinates and approved desk geometry remain protected. The source v0.6.90-dev milestone is **not an accepted release**: full local Node regression QA and actual Ubuntu Electron playthrough are still required.
