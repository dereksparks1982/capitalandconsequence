# Institutional Venture Concept: Blackwood Asylum in Federal Electric
Date: 2026-10-21
Status: *Proposal recorded; business gameplay not authorized/implemented*

## Earlier concepts recovered
- **Blackwood Asylum**: proposed late-19th-century institution-management game (around 1895) with a superintendent's office, budgets, personal finances, patient and employee histories, admissions, contraband and smuggling, investigations, secrets, loyalties, and the choice to act honestly or corruptly.
- **Moroz Asylum / Лечебница «Мороз»**: separate Soviet-era hospital-management concept placed in the fictional town **Cherny Bor**, with town life beyond the institution and the possibility of government pressure and clandestine experiments. It was set in a **Soviet** world, not Washington in 1890.
- Proposed integration **borrows mechanical ideas from both**, but does not erase the other repo or pretend Soviet government institutions existed in 1890 Washington.

## Federal Electric adaptation
A proposed fictional institution called **Blackwood Asylum** may join the Washington city registry and become a distinct institution rather than a minigame glued to the factory. Its address, whether public/charitable/private, charter, eligibility for acquisition, tax rules and supervisory authority remain under research and player decision.

Possible future involvement:
1. **Financial investor**: buy a defined stake, receive financial reports and bear risk, without automatically commanding doctors.
2. **Superintendent / manager**: appointed leadership with staff, ward operations, procurement, intake, patient care, treatment reports, safety incidents and outside inspections.
3. **Controlling owner**: only if the institution is structured to permit private acquisition, and subject to ongoing care, oversight and staff obligations.

## Gameplay principles
- Separate *institution money and accounting* from **Federal Electric company funds** and personal cash. Ownership, salary, charitable donations, and director compensation follow actual contracts.
- Persistent patients and staff have unique identities, records, changing conditions, choices, complaints, family contacts, transfers and outcomes. Care, abuse, negligence and hidden corruption should have meaningful simulated consequences and investigations rather than treating patients as interchangeable resources.
- Settings, technology, diagnoses and political pressure should follow 1890 alternate-Washington plausibility. A fictional institution can have morally complex scandals, contraband and cover stories, but the exact hospital practices require research.
- Roster, chart, superintendent desk, departmental tabs, event inbox, case archive, negotiations, city interactions and external inspections can reuse Federal Electric's modular UI/clock framework.
- A future building or property may become a 13th+ map pin **only after its geographic site is chosen**; its unpinned planned-only room registry currently blocks ordinary entry.
- Autosave all financial changes, appointments, patient-care results and investigations as persistent events. No reloading to erase mistakes.

## Implemented in v0.6.13
- Proposed institutional location shell, not accessible as a finished room.

- Read-only Info view and a data catalog listing possible involvement types **all disabled**.
- No patient data, ownership transaction, price, staffing, medical simulation or investments implemented.
