## Starting life

Eli works as an **anonymous employee watching the door of a Washington nightclub**. The proprietor barely knows he exists, and Eli has no share in the property, office privileges or criminal organization. The internal venue ID remains **`secondNightclub`**, but the public venue name is **Le Paon d’Or (The Golden Peacock)**. Its owner is **Lucien Étienne “Lui” Delacroix**, whose name is approved.

Eli has his own **personal desk and work ledger**, and a physically reachable **Doorman’s Station** inside the registered nightclub. A public main room is available to ordinary visitors; the proprietor's private office remains restricted to management.

## Current gameplay

At Washington Night, Eli can work up to four paid entrance segments per game date. Each segment involves ten minutes watching the entrance, a saved visitor situation, a choice lasting another five minutes, and a provisional **$0.30 wage** credited to his personal cash upon completion only. His total paid time and journal ledger survive autosaving. He can leave work and pursue personal interests elsewhere in Washington.

The first visitors are authored, deterministic workplace encounters. They do not imply a fully autonomous clientele, membership decision algorithm or criminal story, and no speculative earlier proposed proprietor name is treated as settled.

## Possible directions, not forced outcomes

Eli might remain a doorman indefinitely, seek promotion, save for a respectable independent business, establish personal friendships, gamble or drift into the underworld through later earned contacts. No predetermined rise to mob leadership or criminal affiliation is imposed.
