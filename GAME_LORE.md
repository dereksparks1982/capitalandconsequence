## APPROVED WORLD AND STORY INTERPRETATIONS

### Alternate history is not a universal override
The story begins in an alternate 1878 Washington, and the owner may authorize inventions, institutions, or events earlier than their real-world dates, including ideas historically associated with later decades. **Every such authorization is specific.** A developer must never infer that *all* dates and gates are now optional.

### The World's Fair is part of the story
**November 1, 2026 owner-approved revision:** Most of alternate Washington already has electric power in 1878. The District streetlighting contract precedes the late-1878 World's Fair. The old 1893 Fair date and after-Fair streetlight contract are superseded. The exact opening date and contract awards are still undecided. This is a specific approved revision, not general permission to rewrite other stories.

**The World's Fair opening date is not yet settled.** Preserve the approved late-1878 placement, streetlighting-before-Fair sequence, and established narrative gates. Do not invent a confirmed opening date, open the event prematurely, or change its consequences without the owner's specific approval. A provisional map access boundary is not the Fair's announced opening day.

A location's map visibility or general visitability is **not** permission to trigger a gated event or skip its story. Being able to visit a mapped destination does not automatically mean its fairgrounds event, ceremonies, attractions, characters, quests, or time-limited activities have begun. Preserve that distinction wherever it is present in existing canon.

### The world, map, cast, and UI are not disposable
- Preserve the owner's **single approved Washington map image across every playable year**. Never replace, redraw, automatically reposition, or date-swap the artwork.
- Keep owner-set location coordinates, approved pin behavior, and user-approved UI geometry unless specifically directed otherwise.
- Preserve the four protagonist identities, careers, relationships, private knowledge, progression, and story arcs. Do not grant one character another's knowledge through private player books or developer shortcuts.
- Preserve political canon, location and institution rules, NPC behavior boundaries, and explicitly approved narrative choices.
- An individual feature request does not silently revoke any of these protections.


## THE WHITCOMB FAMILY AND FEDERAL ELECTRIC · NOVEMBER 2, 2026

**Approved by the owner:** Charles “Charlie” Whitcomb has a **British father and an Egyptian mother**. He comes from a wealthy family established in Alexandria, Egypt. His father served previously in the **British military elsewhere, NOT in Egypt**, then built a successful **overland trading network** in Egypt: warehouses, land-based transport and merchant relationships, not a sea-freight empire. The elder Whitcomb is a strict, orderly, drill-sergeant-like former officer who loves and wants the best for his family but treats commercial commitments seriously. He is emotionally reserved, demanding, business-minded and generally well-intentioned, **not a cartoon villain**. His exact name, military rank and deployment, his wife's name, and their religions have **not** been decided. Britain did not militarily occupy Egypt until 1882; the family business may precede that occupation through already-existing commercial ties.

Charlie travels **from Alexandria toward Washington**, where he opens the modest Federal Electric electrical-component factory with family backing. The **American factory, property, equipment and working capital were financed, not gifted**. His father deliberately makes a calculated financial investment in Charlie's success. The existing January 1878 campaign still begins at the real Federal Electric factory. **A four-scene, choice-driven Alexandria departure and passenger-cabin introductory vignette is now implemented for newly created Charlie profiles** (v0.7.92-dev), preserving the opening game minute, property, cash and equity. This is not yet an actual playable Alexandria harbor or a time-charged, charted voyage; the approved shipping chart must not be altered just to invent Alexandria coordinates.

**Separate founding obligations:** The financing consists of a real **secured business loan** requiring regular monthly repayment and a **separate original minority equity holding for his father**. At the first implemented balance, Charles has **7,500 of 10,000 voting shares (75%)** and the father holds **2,500 (25%)**. The proposed initial loan is **$7,500 at 4% simple annual interest charged monthly with a $45 installment**, modeled as a provisional gameplay economic scale, not an authenticated 1878 family contract or a second cash grant. Loan repayment **does not** eliminate the father's voting ownership, dividends or designated board seat. A future IPO preserves those shares as real holdings and dilutes percentages when new shares are issued. The father is a voting investor; corporate board elections and board votes remain distinct from shareholder votes.

A prolonged, unremedied secured-loan default may lead to warnings, a formal demand, **foreclosure of the pledged factory's management rights**, and disownment. Charlie's character and personal legal shares must not disappear by narrative magic; the game remains playable after losing management, with any genuine new career/factory-repurchase branches to be implemented later. A parent can be demanding while still wanting his son to succeed; outcomes can include respect, reconciliation, financial independence, failure or estrangement depending on genuine actions.

**Other protagonist origins (approved direction, introductions not yet implemented):** Edmund Wardman begins his backstory in **London**; Samuel Harlan in **southern Africa** (specific birthplace and journey not yet fixed); Elias Crowe was **born and raised in Washington, D.C.**, with **no overseas arrival**. These are future character-opening design directions, **not** permission to bypass the existing January 1878 careers, their locations, or time gates.

**Separate future design, not part of the current loan build:** Different maritime-port icon treatments and merchant contacts may reflect **local earned reputation and honorable or dishonest conduct**, inspired by honor-gated missions. Existing physical ports remain the same positions, and no moral label is assigned to every inhabitant of a port. The elder Whitcomb's Alexandria land-trade contacts could provide earned introductions and mutually accountable shipping contracts **once an Alexandria port and actual character travel have been expressly integrated**. Do not grant all ocean ports, free cargo, or private contacts merely because Charlie is related to him.

### Aboard the moving passenger vessel · v0.7.93-dev
An implemented development-source vessel interior now allows Charles to walk across small top-down deck maps while the **previously built** maritime passenger route advances on the original saved Washington world clock. Both steamer and schooner have accessible berth, galley, water, card-table and lookout where available. Stair hatches connect neighboring decks and crew-only areas remain closed. Casual twenty-one is played for no money; named/independently scheduled passengers and substantial shipboard social storylines are still open. The ship is a moving location, never a separate timer or a grant of shipping-company ownership. **This is not yet the actual Alexandria-origin introduction:** Charlie's existing opening remains four authored choice scenes. An explorable Alexandria waterfront and properly planned Egypt-to-America passenger voyage require further work.

### Walkable Alexandria and anonymous shipmates · v0.7.94-dev
A **source-only staged Alexandria neighborhood** now exists within Charlie's four-scene introductory chapter. It represents the approved family overland trade premises, courtyard, land storehouse and departure waterfront using simple traversable squares, with a cabin floor during the third story chapter. The prelude can be walked and inspected but **does not advance actual world time, issue paid passage or send Charlie along the maritime chart**; the original Federal Electric starting minute on January 2, 1878 remains unchanged. This is a first physical-location layer, NOT the full starting city or actual route from Egypt to America.

The separately implemented full-clock passenger-voyage system now depicts three anonymous shipboard people with position and nearby conversation driven solely by that voyage's authoritative saved minute. These are deliberately unconfirmed traveler identities, not additions to the established named Washington cast or invented family contacts. Actual face-to-face dialogue is saved; no independent clock or payments are created.
