## Unbreakable project decisions

**Develop Washington, D.C., first.** All 26 local locations, their room shells, neighborhood life, factory, clubs, police, asylum and NPC systems take precedence over a nation-wide map or additional cities. The external country is interesting **background lore**; document later-national work on the roadmap only until the owner explicitly widens scope.

**Alternate reality, recognizable but different.** The real American Civil War occurred **1861–1865**. **After** the war, this game's United States abolished states and the old state borders, replacing them with purpose-based districts. The capital remains Washington. Agriculture and husbandry provide the breadbasket; industry, minerals, timber, maritime shipping, transportation, commerce and governance are other provisional district functions. **Their names/borders/election system are NOT final.** A read-only **District Register** lives on the main title screen; it is not a travel map. The *Rick and Morty* comparison refers to the **alternate-universe feel only**, not a crossover. Familiar American details do not automatically mean identical real-world laws, maps or outcomes.
