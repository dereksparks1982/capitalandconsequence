Controls

Walk: WASD or arrow keys move one tile per press. Click a clear destination for an instant, path-checked move. Click an adjacent door to open or close it.

Edit: Pick an object on the left, then click a tile. Select / Move uses one click to select and another to place it. Press R to rotate, Delete to remove, or Ctrl+Z to undo.

Floor and wall painting: Single tile, bucket fill, or two-corner rectangle. Use Erase to remove walls; floors stay filled.

EXPERIMENTAL BRANCH ONLY. Test Center uses its own browser storage key. It never alters the real game's room layouts, world time, finances, characters, or saves. Design ideas were studied from Porcupine, Space Station 14, Ogmo Editor, 2dtiler, and Open World Builder; no third-party code or artwork is bundled.
