## v0.5.0-dev direction

Federal Electric is expanding from a factory/business simulation into a Washington influence and power-broker life simulation without abandoning the company.

Long arc:
- owner/operator of Federal Electric
- delegated factory management
- Washington social/influence network
- political contacts and appointments
- alternate-history presidentially appointed Mayor of Washington, D.C. path
- city administration while Federal Electric continues under fallible management

Alternate-history rule:
- in the Federal Electric timeline, Washington's early presidentially appointed mayoralty was never abolished
- a sufficiently connected player may eventually seek appointment from the President
- this is an explicit fictional divergence from real D.C. history
