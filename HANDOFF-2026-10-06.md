## Accepted player radio baseline

The bottom radio control is now a normal player-facing system rather than a DEV feature.

Control layout:
1. previous station
2. station frequency/name readout
3. next station
4. volume down
5. volume up
6. numeric volume
7. power

Behavior:
- The radio remains fixed on screen while the page itself scrolls.
- The frequency/name readout is the drag handle for the whole radio on PC and touch devices.
- On desktop that drag area uses the same four-direction **move** cursor as the DEV-panel header.
- Volume moves in 5-point steps.
- Fresh desktop/page load starts at **75%**.
- Fresh small-screen/mobile load starts at **50%**.
- Volume is session-only and is intentionally not written to profile saves or localStorage.
- Old floating station-name labels above the desk radio are retired.
- The old speaker-area radio power hotspot is retired.
- The physical desk-radio tuning dial remains interactive.
- The DEV panel contains no duplicate radio station/power/volume controls.

- city/street ambience is suppressed
- fan audio is suppressed
- clock ticking is suppressed
- radio/music remains available

Desktop title audio retains the richer room ambience.
