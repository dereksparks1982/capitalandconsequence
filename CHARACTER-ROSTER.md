# Character Roster · Capital & Consequence

**Status:** Working source + approved story decisions as of October 24, 2026. **Names are not proof of complete AI routines or historical authenticity.** Use this register before naming any NPC. Entries labeled *planned* are not coded yet.

## Four protagonists

- **Charles “Charlie” Whitcomb**: Federal Electric's owner, not a salaried executive.
- **Samuel “Sam” Harlan**: Black rookie patrolman, assigned to Hillsdale/Barry Farm on duty; off-duty free to visit Washington normally.
- **Elias “Eli” Crowe**: lowly nightclub doorman employed by Lui; not automatically a criminal.
- **Edmund Wardman**: lowly orderly at fictional **District Asylum**; unnamed older brother runs the institution; no automatic management power.

Other three continue their own autonomous histories during a single-character playthrough. Routes do not predetermine whether anyone becomes a criminal, investigator, politician or institution head.

## Historical people (real biographies, **fictional** alternate-1878 jobs where stated)

**Samuel J. Tilden**: president of the game's 1878 United States, having won its alternate 1876 nationwide popular vote. **Now a scheduled NPC** at the existing White House for the 1877–1887 first term; future reelections remain undecided.

**Thomas A. Hendricks**: Tilden's actual running mate, **proposed** game vice president.

**Abram S. Hewitt**, **John Bigelow**, **Manton Marble**, **Daniel Manning**, **William T. Pelton**: Tilden's historical political circle and possible advisers/officeholders. **George W. Smith** is a *provisional* private-secretary name awaiting documentary verification. Their exact fictional appointments are not all canon.

**Thomas F. Bayard, Winfield Scott Hancock, Allen G. Thurman, Horatio Seymour, Samuel J. Randall**: prominent Democratic figures, potential rivals, congressional contacts or appointment candidates.

**James G. Blaine, Roscoe Conkling, Blanche K. Bruce, Robert Smalls, Frederick Douglass**: political and public figures who may be encountered in Washington. Douglass's actual 1877 marshal appointment was made by **Hayes**, not Tilden; his alternative job remains unselected.

**William Wilson Corcoran**: wealthy banker/philanthropist. **James Wormley**: Black Washington hotel proprietor, excellent candidate for personally interactable NPC.

The president's circle should **never** be depicted as physically present at every poker match. Real lives, occupations, locations, access and meeting schedules matter. See [Political Washington](POLITICAL-WASHINGTON.md).

## Data fidelity

Aliases and full names belong in the NPC registry and should appear consistently in all UI, profiles, dialogue and notes **when implemented**. The route keys `investigator` and `criminal` do not decide a protagonist's morality or starting job. Preserve period gating, save identity, earned knowledge and the existing fixed map. Standard playing-card-sized character reference cards are a future presentation option, not a build prerequisite.
