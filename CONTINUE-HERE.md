## Invariants that must never be guessed away

1. **Time:** one real second of active/unpaused play = one game second. The world clock ticks even if the player does nothing. When the player performs a timed action (e.g., 3 or 6 hours visiting **Madam Star's Club**, or 1 or 8 hours playing cards), the action **adds its explicit game duration** to that same saved clock. Characters can also travel, work, investigate, rest or sleep. Time is not a mandatory real-time 24-hour session.
2. **NPC synchronization:** **only the selected playable character's ongoing time and actions move the master clock**. Unselected protagonists and NPCs live their lives *within* each already elapsed interval, updating their real locations, actions, cash, relationships, memories and consequences. NPC actions must NEVER independently cause extra master-clock time jumps. They are not merely teleported to a future waypoint without intervening life simulation; current slot-based NPC simulation is partial and must be deepened.
3. **No fake rhythms:** the owner explicitly **rejected** the assistant-invented 60-real-minute business day + 60-real-minute night / 9x daytime + 15x nighttime scheme implemented in v0.6.70. Do not restore. No automatic timer expiration and no forced end-of-day click. Time is frozen when the game is closed, hidden, paused or Developer Freeze Time enabled; there is **no offline catch-up**.
4. **One sky:** use bundled licensed GitHub **Astronomy Engine** for sunrise/sunset, moon phases and solar/lunar eclipses; use the single saved Washington date/time. Small weather and sun/moon icons live in the top HUD. Dynamic weather is an incomplete area. NPCs should realistically know current weather and react. Eclipse-driven crime and genuine detective detection are **future emergent possibilities** based on facts/evidence, not mandatory killings or supernatural insight.
5. **Open conversation:** user wants **actual unscripted local AI conversation**, not only dialogue trees. Residents should speak with personality and language limitations, memories, time-gated period knowledge and social consequences. The source has an experimental OpenAI-compatible loopback model bridge, but **no bundled tested model/weights**. Evaluate reusable **GitHub repositories** (AI Town, Generative Agents, llama.cpp, Ollama, Astronomy Engine and others where useful) instead of reinventing established systems; check licenses. Game size of `~100 GB` is acceptable if useful; measure RAM/VRAM, test real language model quality. Talking must eventually trigger **verified physical actions**, not just NPC prose promises.
6. **Languages and evidence:** Alexi Rurik speaks only Russian; Pavel Orlov is provisional Russian/English. Other residents require real mutual speech/interpreters. Separate personal knowledge, witnessed facts, hearsay, public newspaper dispatches and hidden truth. No automatic future knowledge, omniscient accusation, magical witness events or false law enforcement authority.
7. **Setting:** alternate America after real 1861–65 Civil War, old states replaced by functional-purpose **districts** with new borders, Electoral College abolished, presidency by **national popular vote**. **Samuel J. Tilden as 1876 winner/1878 president is a working idea, not a finalized sequence of administrations.** World is recognizable but deliberately different, *Washington only* active scope. The 1893 World's Fair is deliberately **in Washington** here, unlike real Chicago, with conditional future Federal Electric bulb contracts and **later separate** streetlight opportunity. Do not put incandescent manufacturing in 1878 without credible gated progress.
8. **Cast:** Charlie Whitcomb owns Federal Electric; Sam Harlan is a Black rookie Hillsdale beat patrolman **not yet a detective**; Eli Crowe is an ordinary doorman **not forced into crime**; Edmund Wardman is a lowly District Asylum orderly whose older **unnamed brother** runs it. All four are playable; non-selected protagonists act autonomously as the city advances. Drek, the secretly wealthy eccentric, may have **rare 1878 steam car** discoverable with his mansion/estate access. **Horse faster for short trips, car faster for longer trips**, without fuel/steam startup/maintenance micro-systems. Exact route crossover is undecided. The mansion and usable steam car are **not built**.
9. **Developer access:** existing floating DEV PANEL should follow all rooms/screens. Dev "Unlock All Rooms" is temporary, player-only, nonpersistent, and does not grant NPC or story permissions. Multiple game profiles autosave. No images/artwork unless specifically requested.
10. **Workflow:** one branch `main`, tested small source slices, no unauthorized unrelated edits, warnings treated as errors, status checked on actual GitHub. **No executable, tag, accepted release or full-project ZIP unless the owner asks.** Changed-files ZIP with SHA-256 may be needed later. **Old-save compatibility is NOT required and must never block the 1878 design.** No old-save migration or compatibility testing is required.
