## Invariants that must never be guessed away

Characters can also travel, work, investigate, rest or sleep. Time is not a mandatory real-time 24-hour session.
2. **NPC synchronization:** **only the selected playable character's ongoing time and actions move the master clock**. Unselected protagonists and NPCs live their lives *within* each already elapsed interval, updating their real locations, actions, cash, relationships, memories and consequences. NPC actions must NEVER independently cause extra master-clock time jumps.
