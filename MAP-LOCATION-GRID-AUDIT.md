# Capital & Consequence · Washington Guide Map Pin Audit
**Updated October 24, 2026. Fixed illustration; 35 numbered locations, including monastery pin 35.**

## Geographic contract

The owner-provided **Washington In and Out Guide** at `assets/artwork/maps/title-map-1938.jpg` is the *only* Washington map. At all dates in this alternate-history game its streets, shorelines, buildings and artwork are identical. We have not replaced, modified, redrawn, or downloaded another map.

The original raster is 2048 × 1159 pixels. Its actual cartographic portion is approximately x=286–1778, y=30–864. **Columns 1–30 and rows A–R** are printed on the illustration; text surrounding the city image is an index, **not land**. Grid references below are calculated from the exact pixel positions on this source image. Grid references mean **where the marker is rendered**, not proof that the destination is historically identified at that cell.

The **right-hand street/location index** and named artwork features guide real placements. The remaining fictional properties need an agreed address or building. A label from the index is evidence; an invented street number is not. Never move a fictional building merely to fill a blank map cell or treat a generic district as a verified front door.

### What has actually been located

- **5 printed features**: recognizable, named buildings or shoreline/canal features depicted on the original drawing. For riverbank and towpath markers the map identifies the feature, *not* a precisely surveyed public gate.
- **16 documented blocks/areas**: known streets, districts or named locations on this map. Includes the **Franciscan Monastery of the Holy Land in America, 1400 Quincy Street NE, Brookland, pin 35, grid 21-E**, in its actual neighborhood but **not falsely claimed to be individually identified by the tiny illustration**.
- **14 locations**: no precise building/lot was specified in the design records. Most are deliberately invented establishments; one is the generic Metropolitan Police destination without a chosen station, and Chain Bridge lies outside the detailed pictured city map. These default pins are only shown in **Edit Pins** mode until the owner places them; their entries remain in the 35-destination directory for review.

### Display and saved-location rules

- Red is for a real destination (e.g., the White House, hospital, monastery, historical community or police institution). Blue is for a **fictional destination**, even when located in an actual landmark (Star Club / Embassy Room).
- Every location retains its existing permanent ID and original pin number, except the additional `franciscanMonastery` with **pin 35**. All remain accessible to the map's search, filters and directory, subject to established travel/room gates. **Religious Sites** is a separate category.
- `federalElectricTitleMapLocationsV2:<profile>` stores the player's personal renames and placements. Old untouched V2 defaults are recognized and updated to the improved map references. Any actually dragged pin stays put. Reset Pins resets positions but **not names**. **Export Pins** exports the profile's exact JSON data.
- Clicking the name input now stays inside the location card without initiating a pointerdown map drag; the **existing viewport zoom and pan code remains unchanged**.
- A dashed border means an address-area approximation. Directory-only sites remain explicit instead of incorrectly appearing on water or the guide's text index.
- The historical Babcock Lake date gate remains intact after 1888. The fixed 1938 illustration does **not** magically show the former filled lake. No other institution received an invented opening date.

## Work still dependent on owner choice

Exact lots for 14 not-yet-established destinations cannot be determined from a historical map because some venues were **invented for the game** and the game's own records explicitly leave their addresses undecided. These are not failures of grid arithmetic. To avoid guessing, their locations are selectable in the map directory, and their existing room IDs and stories remain intact.
